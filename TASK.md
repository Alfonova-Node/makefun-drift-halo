## Game design (make it excellent — this is judged by humans and AI judges)
"Drift Halo": you pilot a small ship in a slowly rotating nebula arena.
- Physics: inertia + drift; space feels heavy; a gravity well at the center pulls debris toward it
- Goal: collect glowing scrap rings that orbit the well; each full orbit ring you clear raises the danger tier
- Enemies: hunters that spawn from the rim, each tier adds a new enemy behavior (straight, orbiting, splitting, homing)
- Player tool: a tether beam (hold mouse button) that can latch onto scrap and whip it into hunters for kills; release to let it fly
- Juice: screen shake, particles, chromatic pulse on tier-up, WebAudio-synthesized sfx (no assets), palette shifts per tier
- Meta: score, best score in localStorage, pause (P/Esc), restart (R), 60fps target
- Title screen explains controls in 3 lines; death screen shows score + one line of flavor text

Quality bar: it must feel ALIVE at 10 seconds in and 5 minutes in. Juice > scope.

Do NOT add any network code. Do NOT use any library. Compile with plain tsc (available via npx if needed) but commit both main.ts and out/main.js so index.html works from static serving.
